The question

How can measured synaptic-device behavior be represented mathematically and incorporated into a trainable neural network?

My contribution

  • Built a Python pipeline to convert experimental synaptic-device measurements into fitted mathematical models.
  • Integrated the device models into a multilayer perceptron for MNIST classification.
  • Implemented forward propagation, backpropagation, ReLU activation, and Adam optimization from scratch in Python.

What the work delivers

The project links experimental characterization, parameter estimation, and model implementation in one workflow. The implementation makes the steps of neural-network training explicit.

This work brings a physics-based approach to machine learning: understanding the underlying model, fitting it to measurements, and implementing the training process.
